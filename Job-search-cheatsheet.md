## 3. LinkedIn Posts tab (catch roles before they hit job boards)

On LinkedIn, search these in the **Posts** tab (NOT the Jobs tab). Recruiters and founders often post a role here first.

```
we are hiring data analyst Melbourne
looking for data analyst Melbourne
hiring BI analyst Australia
new grad data analyst 2026 Melbourne
join our team data analyst Melbourne
```

If you see one early, you can comment, message, or ask about it before it has 500 applicants.

## 7. Visa signal (AU version - adapted)

Ignore US systems (H-1B / OPT / STEM OPT / myvisajobs) - those are irrelevant in Australia. Your version is spotting employers comfortable with visa holders / sponsorship.

- Look for JDs that say "full working rights" (your 485 qualifies) rather than "PR/citizen only".
- Note employers known to sponsor (e.g. large firms, some tech). Bookmark them.
- Don't open with "do you sponsor?" - check signals first (do they have visa-holder staff? international alumni?).

```
site:linkedin.com/in "[company name]" "485"
site:linkedin.com/in "[company name]" international graduate Melbourne
```

## 8. Advanced Google operators (cheat codes)

- **site:** - search one website
  `site:boards.greenhouse.io data analyst Melbourne`
- **intitle:** - keyword must be in the page title
  `intitle:careers data analyst Melbourne`
- **inurl:** - keyword must be in the URL
  `inurl:careers data analyst Melbourne`
- **OR** - search several options at once
  `data analyst OR BI analyst OR reporting analyst Melbourne`
- **minus (-)** - exclude words (cut senior roles)
  `data analyst Melbourne -senior -manager -lead`
- **after:** - only results after a date (fresh roles)
  `data analyst Melbourne after:2026-06-15`

Combine them:
```
site:boards.greenhouse.io ("data analyst" OR "BI analyst") Melbourne -senior -manager after:2026-06-01
```

## The flow that actually works (from the guide, and it's right)

Finding the role early is only step one. Then:
1. Read the JD carefully.
2. Tailor your resume to the job's exact keywords (ATS match).
3. Find 1-2 people connected to the company and reach out.
4. Apply within the first 24-72 hours (early applicants get read).
5. Send a short follow-up if you have a warm connection there.

Late applications become one line in an ATS. Early + keyword-matched resume + an insider who's seen your name = a genuinely different outcome.
